FAQ

Straight answers.

How does bundle pricing work?

The first agent is $299 per month, the second is $199, and each agent after that is $99. The CRM is included from two agents on. Full Front Desk AI is $799 for all five agents, the CRM, and the Online Scheduler. Workflow / Automation is an extra $99 per month. These are draft prices. CRM is included with any two or more agents.

What is the texting allowance?

Plans with texting include a monthly text allowance. Texts beyond that allowance are billed per message. Allowance and overage amounts will be confirmed in your proposal.

Do I need the CRM?

Recall, Treatment, Waitlist, and Review require the CRM (CRM); Call Agent does not. CRM is included with 2+ agents. With one agent it is +$199, required for those four agents and optional with Call Agent. CRM without agents is $399 per month.

Will the agent actually book appointments?

Yes, on Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. On other systems it captures the patient and hands your team a ready-to-book lead.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time Trackable setup fee. Amount TBD, billed separately from the monthly subscription.
