FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need the CRM?

Only for recall, unscheduled treatment, waitlist gap-fill, and reviews, since those need patient data. Never miss a call works without it.

Why do some outcomes say $TBD?

Prices for those outcomes have not been set yet.

Will the agent actually book appointments?

Yes, on Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. On other systems it captures the patient and hands your team a ready-to-book lead.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time Trackable setup fee, separate from LaunchPad.
