FAQ

Straight answers.

What's the difference between the tiers?

Starter is the Call Agent. Growth adds the Recall Agent, the Review Agent, the Trackable CRM, and the Online Scheduler. Accelerate is Full Front Desk AI: all six agents, the CRM, and the Online Scheduler.

How much does Accelerate save?

Bought one piece at a time on modular pricing, everything in Accelerate would come to $1,213 a month. Accelerate is $799, a $414 monthly savings. Draft prices.

Do I need a different agent for missed calls and new patients?

No. It's the same Voice Agent. Point it at missed calls, after-hours calls, new-patient calls, or all of them; where it sits in your call tree doesn't change the product.

Will the agent actually book appointments?

Yes, on Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. On other systems it captures the patient and hands your team a ready-to-book lead.

Just need the CRM?

Yes. Trackable CRM on its own is $299 / location / month. You can add a plan later.

What does it cost to text patients?

Each plan includes a monthly text allowance. Beyond that, texts are billed per message, because carriers charge us for every SMS.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time Trackable setup fee covers onboarding, call routing, and PMS connection. It's separate from LaunchPad, which is an SMC program.

How is pricing counted for groups?

Per location, per month. Each office that uses a plan or the CRM counts as one location.
