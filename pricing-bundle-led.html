FAQ

Straight answers.

What's in the full setup?

The Voice Agent (missed calls, after hours, and new patient scheduling, wherever you point it), plus the full Trackable CRM and the Online Scheduler.

How is the agent priced?

A monthly base per location plus a per-call rate for calls it handles. Both amounts are drafts and still to be set. The CRM ($199) and Scheduler ($199) are flat per location.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. Start with the agent alone and add the CRM or Scheduler later, or start with CRM only ($399) and add the agent when you're ready.

Will it book appointments?

Yes, on Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. On other systems it captures the patient for your team to book.

What about texting costs?

A monthly text allowance is included. Extra messages are billed per message, because carriers charge us for every SMS.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time Trackable setup fee, billed separately. It's not LaunchPad, which is an SMC program.

Is Advanced PMS included?

No, it's a +$50 add-on on any setup.
