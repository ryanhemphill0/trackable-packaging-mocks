Internal notes

Why this layout hurts

What a practice owner runs into trying to buy from this page.

Features, not outcomes

SKU codes describe plumbing ("VOICE-NPA") and the card lists features instead of what happens ("every missed or after-hours new-patient call answered and booked").

Prices contradict each other

CRM appears at $399 and again as a $199 "combo" line. Buyers anchor on the cheapest and distrust the rest.

No obvious starting point

Five line items, no recommendation, no suggested pairing. Every deal becomes a sales-assisted configuration.

Usage cost is invisible

The agent is a monthly base plus per call, but there's no example bill or estimator, so buyers can't predict what they'll pay.

Dependencies are hidden

Does Scheduler need CRM? Does Advanced PMS need an agent? The page doesn't say.

Routing is hidden

Nothing says the one agent can cover missed calls, after hours, or all new-patient scheduling. Buyers assume they need different products for each.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which products do I need?

Contact sales to configure a package for your practice. [Note: this is the problem.]

Can I combine combos with other products?

Combos cannot be combined with other offers. [Note: unclear to buyers.]

Does the agent book appointments?

On Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. Other PMS are capture-only.

What does the agent cost?

A monthly base plus a per-call charge. Contact sales. [Note: amounts are drafts, to be set, and the page gives no example bill.]

Are texts included?

An allowance is included; overage is billed per message, since carriers charge per SMS with or without an agent.

Fine print (applies to every plan on this page)
1
Per location, per month. All prices are drafts, quoted per practice location per month. Annual billing saves roughly 20–25%.
2
Agent pricing. The Voice Agent is one agent: missed-call, after-hours, and new-patient handling are where it sits in your call tree, not separate products. It is billed as a monthly base per location plus a per-call usage rate for calls it handles. Both amounts are drafts, to be set.
3
Texting. Every plan includes a monthly text allowance. Messages beyond it are billed per message, because we pay carriers for every SMS, whether or not an agent sent it.
4
One-time setup. A Trackable setup fee is billed separately and covers onboarding, call routing, and PMS connection. This is Trackable's own setup, not LaunchPad (LaunchPad is an SMC program).
5
Booking depends on your PMS. The agent books directly into supported systems. On any other PMS, it captures the patient and hand off to your team to book.
