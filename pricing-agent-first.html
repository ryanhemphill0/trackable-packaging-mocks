FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need the CRM to use an agent?

No. The Voice Agent and Full Front Desk both work on their own and include a basic inbox. Add the CRM when you want full lead tracking and to see which marketing brings in patients.

Will the agent actually book appointments?

Yes, on Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, CareStack, and Dentrix Ascend. On other systems it captures the patient and hands your team a ready-to-book lead.

What does it cost to text patients?

Each plan includes a monthly text allowance. Beyond that, texts are billed per message, because carriers charge us for every SMS.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time Trackable setup fee covers onboarding, call routing, and PMS connection. It's separate from LaunchPad, which is an SMC program.

How is pricing counted for groups?

Per location, per month. Each office that uses an agent or the CRM counts as one location.

Do I need a different agent for missed calls and new patients?

No. It's the same Voice Agent. Point it at missed calls, after-hours calls, new-patient calls, or all of them; changing where it sits in your call tree doesn't change the product.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move between the Voice Agent and Full Front Desk as your needs change.
